## The entry

`main.py` writes one sentence to `/output/entry.txt` — a single sentence of
176 characters that explains the modern internet to someone who has just
stepped out of the year 1800, using only things a person from that year
already knows: a road, a post office, a library, a market, a letter, ink.

> It is a single road joining every library, post office and market on earth, so a letter sent in London is read in Canton before its ink dries, and any stranger may answer back.

## Why this sentence

- **It explains what the internet is and does.** One road joining every
  library, post office, and market on earth says what it is; a letter read
  across the world before its ink dries, answered back by any stranger,
  says what it does.
- **It is built from 1800.** A road, a post office, a library, a market,
  a letter, ink — nothing in it needs a word coined after the traveler's
  time.
- **It is true.** The internet really does join the world's stores of
  knowledge, mail, and trade, and really does carry a message across the
  world near-instantly, open to anyone to answer.
- **One image, remembered.** A letter from London read in Canton before
  its ink dries is the whole internet in a single picture.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt (in the grader sandbox)
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py   # local test: writes out/entry.txt
```
